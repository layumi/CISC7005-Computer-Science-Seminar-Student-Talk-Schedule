## How the schedule works

- **One group per slot.** A group of 1 student gets a **15-minute** slot. A group of 2 students gets a **20-minute** slot. Q\&A is inside the slot.
- **The first group starts at 14:00 sharp.** There is no opening.
- Arrive at least 10 minutes before your slot and be ready to present earlier than listed.
- Order within each session is by **paper ID**, not by importance.
- One 10-minute break per session, marked `BREAK` in the tables.
- The order is final. A swap is possible only if both groups agree and confirm with me before 9 Nov.

## Checklist for presenters

- [ ] Slides in PDF, submitted at least 24 hours before your slot.
- [ ] About 80% figures, 20% text; font size no smaller than 24 pt.
- [ ] Structure: title and 3–5 contributions, related work with a comparison table, method, experiments, discussion and limits, conclusion and 3 thought questions.
- [ ] Include a reflection on the official ICLR reviews: top 2–3 reviewer comments, where you agree or disagree, and how you would improve the paper.
- [ ] Re-implement or run the released code if available, and check the results yourself.
- [ ] Be in the room 10 minutes before your slot. English fluency is not graded.
